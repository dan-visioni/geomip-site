GeoMIP 2021 Meeting: Online

Eleventh GeoMIP Workshop

Online

Thursday, July 8 through Friday, July 9, 2021

Workshop summary (published in BAMS)


Due to COVID-19, the 11th GeoMIP meeting was held online, out of concern for the safety of all participants.

The meeting was held on July 8th and July 9th over Zoom. The program can be found here.

A recording of the meeting and slides from the speakers are available to participants.

Workshop Organizers: Alan Robock (Rutgers University) and Daniele Visioni (Cornell University)
