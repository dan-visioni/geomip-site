GeoMIP Support

Coordination

Partial support for coordination of GeoMIP is provided by U.S. National Science Foundation grants AGS-1617844 and AGS-2017113.
Additional support for coordination and website hosting are provided by the Department of Environmental Sciences at Rutgers University.
GeoMIP is an endorsed community coordinated project by the Working Group on Coupled Modeling (WGCM) of the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP).

Computer Modeling and Analysis

BNU-ESM

Participation in GeoMIP is supported by the Cooperation Centre of Global Change at Beijing Normal University. GeoMIP data set publishing is supported by the Centre of Information and Network Technology at Beijing Normal University.

CSIRO-Mk3L

Participation in GeoMIP is supported by the CSIRO Wealth from Oceans Flagship and the Australian Research Council Centre of Excellence for Climate System Science, with the assistance of resources provided at the Australian National University through the National Computational Merit Allocation Scheme supported by the Australian Government.

GISS-E2-R

GISS-E2-R simulations were supported by the NASA High-End Computing (HEC) Program through the NASA Center for Climate Simulation (NCCS) at Goddard Space Flight Center.

HadGEM2-ES

The work with HadGEM2-ES was supported by the Joint UK DECC/Defra Met Office Hadley Centre Climate Programme (GA01101).

IPSL

Participation in GeoMIP is supported by the EU 7th Framework Programme under grant agreement 306395, EuTRACE. IPSL-CM5A-LR simulations were supported through HPC resources of [CCT/TGCC/CINES/IDRIS] under the allocation 2012-t2012012201 made by GENCI (Grand Equipement National de Calcul Intensif). IPSL-CM5A-LR and IPSL-CM6A-LR simulations were supported through HPC resources of TGCC allocations 2016-A0030107732, 2017-R0040110492 and 2018-R0040110492 made by GENCI (Grand Equipement National de Calcul Intensif).

MIROC-ESM

Modeling efforts for MIROC-ESM and MIROC-ESM-CHEM are supported by the SOUSEI program, MEXT, Japan. The Earth Simulator was used for simulations.

MPI-ESM-LR

Participation in GeoMIP was supported by the European Comission's 7th Framework Program through the IMPLICC project (FP7-ENV-2008-1-226567) and under grant agreement 306395, EuTRACE. Computational resources have been provided by DKRZ (Deutsches Klima Rechenzentrum).

NorESM1-M

Participation in GeoMIP was supported by the European Commission's 7th Framework Program: the IMPLICC project (FP7-ENV-2008-1-226567) and the EuTRACE project (grant agreement 306395). NorESM1-M work has also been funded by the EXPECT project: EXPECT project, grant 229760/E10, funded by the Norwegian Research Council. Computational resources have been provided by Notur, supported by the Norwegian Research Council.
