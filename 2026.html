GeoMIP 2026 Meeting: Tokyo, Japan

Sixteenth GeoMIP Workshop

17-19 March, 2026

The Sixteenth GeoMIP meeting will be held in Tokyo, Japan from March 17th to March 19th, 2026 at the University of Tokyo.


A block of rooms have been reserved at the Forest Hongo hotel, reach out to the organizers for further info.


Workshop Organizers: Alan Robock (Rutgers University) and Daniele Visioni (Cornell University)
Local hosts: Masahiro Sugiyama (University of Tokyo) and Shingo Watanabe (JAMSTEC)



This workshop is partially supported by the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) and the Quadrature Climate Foundation.
