GeoMIP 2014 Meeting: UPMC

Fourth GeoMIP Workshop

University of Pierre and Marie Curie, Paris, France

Thursday, April 24 through Friday, April 25, 2014

Workshop Agenda

Workshop Summary (Published in Eos)


Workshop Organizers: Olivier Boucher (LMD), Bénédicte Fisset (LMD), Ben Kravitz (Pacific Northwest National Laboratory), and Alan Robock (Rutgers University)


This workshop is a continuation of our previous three workshops:
  • First workshop held at Rutgers University in February 2011.
  • Second workshop held at the University of Exeter in March 2012.
  • Third workshop held at the Institute for Advanced Sustainability Studies in April 2013.
Objectives of the Workshop:
  • Discuss past, present, and future of GeoMIP
  • Present results from the three new computer experiments on marine cloud brightening
  • Involvement in interdisciplinary efforts

This workshop is partially supported by U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) grants AGS-1157525 and GEO-1240507.
